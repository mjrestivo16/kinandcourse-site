What a deliverable contains

A transcript formatted to admissions convention, with a documented grading scale and credit summary; course descriptions written to match the coursework; where the package includes it, a year-by-year plan mapped to your state’s requirements and to the published admission requirements of your target colleges. Everything arrives as an editable file plus a print-ready PDF, with sources cited so you can verify the research. The transcript is issued and signed by you, the parent.
