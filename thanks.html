What happens next

A person reads it, then writes back.

  1. Within one business day

    Mark reads your intake and replies personally by email — confirming which package fits your situation, or telling you honestly that you don’t need us. It is a real reply, not an autoresponder.

  2. If it’s a fit

    That reply includes a secure payment link for the flat price you were quoted, and a short list of the records to send over. You decide from there; there is no follow-up sequence and no sales call.

  3. Then the work

    Five to ten business days depending on the package, counted from the day payment clears and your records arrive. One round of revisions is included, and the money-back guarantee stands throughout.

If you don’t hear from us

Check your spam folder first — a reply from an address you have never emailed before is exactly what filters like to catch. If it is not there after one business day, email mark@restivo.net directly and it will get sorted out. Something reaching the wrong folder should never cost you a deadline.
